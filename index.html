How do mediated bodies and environments change the way people perceive and act?

About

I study how mediated bodies and environments in immersive technologies shape human perception and action, and how AI systems can interpret and support these processes.

I am a researcher at the Virtual Reality Lab at Konkuk University, working with Prof. Hyungseok Kim. My M.S. research examined how avatar representation and haptic feedback affect embodiment and behavior in virtual reality.
